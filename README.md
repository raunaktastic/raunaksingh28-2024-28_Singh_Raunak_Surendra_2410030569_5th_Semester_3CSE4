#About Espotz

**Espotz** is a competitive esports tournament platform focused on providing infrastructure for organizing, running, and promoting online gaming tournaments.

The platform includes:

-  Tournament creation and management
-  Player and team onboarding
-  Real-time match results and bracket progression
-  Automated promotional graphics
   Frontend and backend platform services

---

##Internship Focus

The major focus of my internship was:

### 1.Chat Technology Research

I researched and evaluated different approaches for implementing an in-tournament real-time chat system.

The evaluation covered:

- Self-hosted WebSocket solutions
- Managed real-time messaging services
- Third-party chat SDKs
- Latency
- Scalability
- Cost
- Integration effort
- Flexibility and operational overhead

Small proof-of-concept prototypes were developed for promising candidates to evaluate real-world behaviour and integration with the existing platform.

### 2.GFX Design & Templating Research

I also researched and evaluated tools and approaches for creating GFX templates for Espotz's automated graphics pipeline.

The evaluation focused on:

- Design flexibility
- Speed of iteration
- Designer-to-developer handoff
- React integration
- Themeability
- Compatibility with the existing rendering pipeline

Sample templates were prototyped to evaluate the different approaches.
